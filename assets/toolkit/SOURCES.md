# Toolkit icon sources

The technology icons in `icons/` are unmodified SVGs from
[Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0/icons).
Names remain visible beside or beneath every icon, and concepts without an
official product mark are presented as text labels instead.

Devicon's project license and the trademarks represented by individual logos
remain with their respective owners.


V3 adds the same pinned release's `nextjs/nextjs-original.svg`,
`typescript/typescript-original.svg` and
`githubactions/githubactions-original.svg`. They are unmodified local SVG
copies. Icons are consistently sized and paired with visible technology names.
Systems/API concepts and Provider are text labels; no generic logo substitutes
are presented as official technology marks. The groups communicate tools used,
not proficiency scores.
